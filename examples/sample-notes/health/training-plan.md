# 10-week 10k plan

Goal: run the Riverside 10k on 6 December in under 55 minutes. Current easy pace is about 6:10/km.

## Weekly shape
- Tuesday: intervals (6 × 400 m at 5:00/km, 90 s jog between)
- Thursday: 5 km tempo at 5:30/km
- Sunday: long easy run, building from 8 km to 14 km
- Strength: two 20-minute sessions (squats, lunges, calf raises, planks)

## Rules
- No more than 10% more distance week to week.
- Left knee niggle from August: if it aches the next morning, swap Thursday for cycling.
- Taper: halve the volume in the final week.
