- Say your project is "based on Munkymind" or "compatible with Munkymind" in plain text

- Call a fork, product or service "Munkymind" (or something confusingly similar), or use the logo for it

**Forks:** you're welcome to fork. Please rename your project, replace the logo, and remove or replace the branded personality and message packs.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue titled "Trademark".
